# ASTR 201 Course Playbook

*Course identity, math ceiling, and assessment posture for ASTR 201.*

Version: v1.0 • Status: Active • Owner: Instructor

---

## 1) Course Identity

### What ASTR 201 Is
- **Audience:** Pre-major science students
- **Prerequisites:** Algebra II; basic physics comfort assumed
- **Goal:** Learn to infer physical reality from constrained measurements
- **Vibe:** Rigorous, quantitative, but concept-first

### What ASTR 201 Is Not
- Not a plug-and-chug math course
- Not a memorization course
- Not a survey of facts without inference

### Core Message
> Astronomy is the art of inferring physical reality from constrained measurements.

Every lecture and assessment must reinforce: measurement → model → inference.

---

## 2) Math Ceiling (Explicit)

### In Scope
- Algebra and proportional reasoning
- Scientific notation
- Logs (when explicitly explained)
- Basic trigonometry (angles, small-angle approximation)
- Dimensional analysis and unit conversions

### Default Unit System
- **CGS** is the default unit system in ASTR 201.
- If SI units appear, they must be explicitly labeled.

### Conceptual Derivatives (Allowed, Not Assessed)
- Derivatives may appear **conceptually** to explain why scalings exist (e.g., Wien's law, stellar structure scalings).
- Students are **not** responsible for performing calculus on HW or exams.

### Out of Scope
- Full calculus (no derivatives or integrals in assessment requirements)
- Advanced vector calculus
- Multi-variable calculus

---

## 3) Assessment Posture

- Reasoning tools are the core learning targets.
- Problems must always include an interpretation step, not just a number.
- Exams are easier than homework: no ⭐⭐⭐ problems, shorter reasoning chains.

---

## 4) Instructor Workflow (Efficiency)

- Build reading problem banks first.
- Select homework subsets from the reading bank.
- Select exam subsets from homework that are shorter and easier.

---

## 5) Voice and Feedback

- Explanations are narrative and precise.
- Errors are framed as model failures or tool misapplications.
- Feedback emphasizes reasoning and assumptions, not just correctness.
